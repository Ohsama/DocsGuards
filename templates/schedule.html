📋 Scheduling Algorithm
1. Every day is classified as Weekday (Sun–Thu) or Weekend (Fri–Sat).
2. Doctors on leave or inactive are automatically excluded for that day.
3. A Round-Robin queue rotates across the entire month — no doctor gets a second shift until everyone has had one.
4. Holiday history prevents a doctor from repeating the same holiday two years in a row.
5. 2 replacement doctors are assigned per day from the global active pool.

📖 Why History Matters
When a doctor is recorded as having worked a holiday (e.g. Eid Al-Adha Day 1) in year N, the scheduler will automatically exclude them from that same holiday in year N + 1, ensuring fair distribution over time.
⚡ Quick Tip
The fastest way to populate history is to:

  1. Generate a schedule for the current year
  2. Click Commit to History
  3. All holiday assignments are saved automatically
